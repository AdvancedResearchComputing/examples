# Astronomy

## Stars

Stars shine because nuclear fusion in their cores turns hydrogen into helium, releasing energy that eventually leaves the surface as light.

The Sun is a main-sequence star about 4.6 billion years old. Light from its surface takes about eight minutes to reach Earth.

## Black holes

A black hole is a region where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape once it crosses the event horizon.

In 2019 the Event Horizon Telescope published the first image of a black hole's shadow, at the center of the galaxy M87.

## Planets

Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System, with more than twice the mass of all the other planets combined.
