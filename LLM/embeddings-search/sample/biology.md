# Biology

## Cells

Mitochondria produce most of a cell's supply of ATP through cellular respiration.

DNA stores genetic instructions as a sequence of four bases: adenine, thymine, guanine, and cytosine.

## Plants

Photosynthesis uses sunlight to turn water and carbon dioxide into glucose, releasing oxygen as a by-product.

## Immunity

Vaccines train the immune system to recognize a pathogen without causing the disease.
